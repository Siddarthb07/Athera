The practice

Five systems. Each one is scoped on its own, then built into the tools the business already uses.

Lead generation

Inquiries are read, scored, and sent to the person who should reply. WhatsApp included, so a lead is not left in a chat overnight.

Ad generation

Copy and creative from the offer you sell, in variations you can run. No performance number we did not measure.

Conversation

Site and WhatsApp replies for the questions you already answer by hand. Anything uncertain goes to a person.

Reception

Picks up, books the time, and routes the calls it should not decide. Your calendar stays in charge.

Workflows

Sheets, mail, and calendars joined so a follow-up is drafted once and not retyped.
