Services

Five systems, scoped one at a time.

Each one is a job an owner already does by hand. The system lands in WhatsApp, the site, the calendar, Sheets, or Gmail.

Lead generation

Inquiries are read and scored before anyone digs through a chat. The ones worth a reply reach a person, including on WhatsApp, so a lead is not left overnight.

  • Read and score each inquiry
  • Send the useful ones to a person
  • WhatsApp included
  • No second inbox to learn

Ad generation

Drafts come from the offer you actually sell, in a few variations you can approve and run. You sign off on every line. A return figure we did not measure does not go in the ad.

  • Copy from the offer you sell
  • A few variations you can run
  • Nothing ships without approval
  • No invented performance numbers

Conversation

Replies for the questions you already answer by hand, on the site and on WhatsApp. If the answer is not in what you have told us, it says so and hands the thread to a person.

  • Site and WhatsApp
  • The questions you already hear
  • Uncertain answers go to a person
  • It does not invent a policy

Reception

It picks up, offers a time, and books into the calendar you already use. Calls it should not decide are routed to you. The calendar stays in charge of what is free.

  • Offers a time and books it
  • Your calendar stays in charge
  • Hard calls are routed to you
  • No separate booking product

Workflows

The sheet, the mailbox, and the calendar joined so a follow-up is drafted once. Athera’s own outreach works this way: the record lives in Google Sheets, and the next note is drafted in Gmail for a person to send.

  • Google Sheets for the record
  • Gmail for the draft
  • A person still sends it
  • Quoted per job, not as one week
