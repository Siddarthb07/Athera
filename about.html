About

Two founders. Bangalore.

Siddarth Boggarapu and Ariv S started Athera so a smaller business could use a system that, until then, was being sold to companies with a software team.

Why it started

The offer on the market was a demonstration. The inbox, the WhatsApp thread, and the follow-up were still done by hand the next morning.

Athera started from that gap. One named job, then a system left in the sheet, the mailbox, the site, or WhatsApp. If it needs a slide to explain the value, it is the wrong project.

Names of clients stay private unless they ask to be named. A number that was not measured does not go on the page.
