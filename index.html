The practice

Five systems. Built around the business you already run.

We do not sell a platform and leave you to configure it. Each engagement is one system, scoped in writing, and handed over in the tools your team opens every day.

01

Lead generation

Inquiries are read, scored, and placed in front of the person who should answer. Built for the channels you already have, including WhatsApp, so a lead does not sit in a chat until it goes cold.

You keep the relationship. The system only decides which message deserves a human first.

02

Ad generation

Copy and creative drafted from the offer you actually sell, in variations you can run. We will not print a return figure we did not measure on your account.

The drafts are yours to approve. Nothing is published because a model preferred a line.

03

Conversation

A chat on your site or WhatsApp that answers the questions you hear every week, and passes anything uncertain to a person. The point is a reply, not a bot that improvises.

If it does not know, it says so and hands the thread over. That refusal is part of the build.

04

Reception

A front desk that picks up, books the appointment, and routes the calls it should not decide. Your calendar stays the source of truth.

Hours, services, and who should be interrupted are written down before it ever answers.

05

Workflows

The path between the tools you already pay for. A sheet, a mailbox, a calendar, connected so the same follow-up is not typed twice.

Athera’s own outreach runs this way: the relationship lives in Google Sheets, and the next email is drafted in Gmail.

Engagement

How a project actually runs.

01

A call, then a recommendation

We learn how the work happens today. You leave with one system worth building first, not a menu of everything AI can theoretically do.

02

Scope and price, in writing

Nothing starts until the job, the boundary, and the fee are clear. If a request would need a claim we cannot stand behind, we say so before the build.

03

Handover to the founder

Siddarth Boggarapu scopes, builds, and supports the work. After handover you are not writing to a queue. You are writing to the person who made it.

Who it is for

Owner-run businesses, not a department with a lab.

The fit is a company where the owner, or a very small team, still feels the missed lead. A clinic, a studio, a local service, a firm that answers on WhatsApp. If the work is already a staffed operations team with its own software group, you do not need Athera.

01

One job, named

We start from a task someone does every day: reply, book, follow up, or draft. If the task cannot be pointed at, the project does not start.

02

Tools you already open

The site, WhatsApp, Google Sheets, Gmail, a calendar. The system is installed there. There is no second login whose only purpose is to look at Athera.

03

A person at the end of it

Siddarth Boggarapu is the person who scopes it and the person who answers after it ships. Bangalore. You write to siddarthb078@gmail.com, not to a shared inbox with no name.

Handover

What you actually receive.

A finished engagement is a running system plus a short written note. The note says what it will do, what it will refuse, and how to reach the founder if it misbehaves.

01

The system, in place

Not a slide and not a login to a demo. The chat is on your site or WhatsApp. The leads land where you already look. The draft is in the mailbox you send from.

02

The boundary, in writing

What the system is allowed to decide, and what must wait for a person. That boundary is part of the delivery, because a confident wrong answer is worse than a delay.

03

Time, quoted per job

A narrow chat can be short. A workflow that touches several tools takes longer. The duration is in the scope, before work starts. There is no standing promise of “live in a week” for every job.
